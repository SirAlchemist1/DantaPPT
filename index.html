Module 03 · 4 of 4 · Clearing

Where AI helps when a cheque bounces

Before sending

Presenting bank checks image quality, words vs figures, date and signature. Avoidable returns never happen.

After a return

Find patterns in non-cleared cheques: one drawer bouncing again and again; one payee collecting bounced cheques from many drawers.

In a dispute

2025 Banking Offence and Punishment Act amendment: notice of up to 45 days, recorded, then certified. AI assembles the evidence pack in seconds.

The benchmark · India

Continuous cheque clearing since Oct 2025. Since Jan 2026, paying banks must confirm within 3 hours. At 3 hours, human-only checking does not scale.

Module 05 · goAML

goAML is already a small ontology

<report>One filing to FIU-Nepal
<transaction>Each movement of value
<t_from_my_client>Where value came from
<t_to>Where it went
<person | account | entity>What each side points to

FIU-Nepal goAML portal notice

No placeholder values such as "NA" in the swift and institution_code fields of account records: they cause significant data-quality and processing problems.

Bad identifiers break the links between reports. An ontology fixes identifiers at the source.

Module 07 · Guardrails

Guardrails are maker–checker for AI

You already know this principle

Maker–checker and dual control. AI needs the same, built into its architecture. OWASP ranks prompt injection the #1 AI risk.

Filters help. Architecture protects.

What goes in

Input filters catch injection patterns and strip hidden text

Document text is data, never commands

What it may do

Least privilege: reads only what it needs, can never move money

Human approval + call-back on a known number for any payment

What comes out

Output filters block account numbers and unsupported claims

Grounding: cite a source, or say "I don't know"

Log every prompt, document and answer

Module 07 · Banned in Nepal, but not absent

Where AI meets crypto

The law in Nepal

Crypto is prohibited

NRB notices in 2017, 2021 and 2023 prohibit trading, mining and holding virtual currency. No licensed crypto activity exists.

The crime side · Chainalysis 2026

$17 bnstolen in crypto scams and fraud in 2025
4.5×more revenue per scam when it buys AI tools
+1,400%growth in impersonation scams

The defence side

AI traces wallets

Blockchain analytics is network analysis on a public ledger. The same "network beats the silo" idea from Aurora helps trace scam proceeds and hundi-style settlement through crypto.

BIS 2026

The BIS Annual Economic Report 2026 judges today's stablecoins against three tests of sound money: singleness, elasticity and integrity. It finds shortcomings, including "deviations from par value in secondary markets".

A ban stops licensed activity. Does it stop Nepalis being scammed in crypto, or money leaving through it?
